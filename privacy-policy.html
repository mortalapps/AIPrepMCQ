Privacy Policy — AI Prep MCQ

Last updated: April 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AI Prep MCQ ("the app", "we", "our") handles information when you use the app on your Android device. We are committed to a privacy-first approach: the app is designed to function almost entirely offline, collects no personally identifiable information, and does not share your data with third parties.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • Your location or device location data
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Camera, microphone, or media access
  • Contacts or calendar data
  • Usage analytics or behavioural telemetry

The app does not require account creation or login.

1.2 Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data generated by your use of the app is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. This includes:

  • Test history: Your scores, answers, and completion timestamps
  • Performance data: Per-question accuracy tracking
  • XP and level progression: Experience points, current level, daily XP
  • Streak data: Daily usage streak count
  • Preferences: Notification settings, premium status, app settings
  • Purchase token: Stored securely on your device to verify premium purchase

No data from the above is ever uploaded or shared with any third party.

2. Permissions Used

Permission Purpose
INTERNET Used only to open the question reporting form (Google Forms). The app does not use internet access for tracking, analytics, or data collection.
com.android.vending.BILLING Required to process the one-time premium purchase via Google Play.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Enables optional daily reminders and notifications (can be disabled).
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Used to reschedule reminders after device restart.

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google Play Billing

Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive a purchase token to verify your purchase and do not process payment details. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.2 Google Forms (Question Reporting)

If you choose to report a question, a Google Form opens inside the app. The form may be pre-filled with question details. Submitting the form sends information directly to Google.

Any information you enter is submitted by you. This feature is optional. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.3 No Analytics, No Advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, and no crash reporting services. We do not collect or transmit any usage data.

4. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is stored locally on your device using secure storage
  • Sensitive data is stored using Android’s encrypted storage
  • No cloud backup is performed
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all data permanently

5. Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no personal data is collected, the app is safe for all age groups.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the date above. Continued use of the app indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
