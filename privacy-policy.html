Privacy Policy — AI Prep MCQ

Last updated: April 24, 2026

AI Prep MCQ ("the app") is designed with a privacy-first approach. The app primarily functions offline and does not require account creation or personal data submission.

1. Information Handling Overview

1.1 Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or process personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location.

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Your activity within the app is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. This includes test history, scores, XP, levels, streaks, and bookmarks.

1.3 Anonymous Reports

If you choose to report a question, the app may send a report containing the question ID and your feedback. Reports are not linked to any personal identifier and are used solely to improve question quality.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Any minimal processing of anonymous technical data (such as question reports) is carried out under our legitimate interest in improving the quality and accuracy of the app.

5. Data Retention

All user data is stored locally on your device and remains until you delete the app or clear its data. We do not store user data on external servers.

6. User Rights (GDPR)

As we do not collect personal data, most rights under the GDPR (such as access, correction, or deletion of personal data) do not apply. However, users may contact us for any privacy-related concerns.
