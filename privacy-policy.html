Privacy Policy — AI Prep

Last updated: May 27, 2026

AI Prep ("the app") is provided by MortalApps ("we", "us", "our"). The app is designed with a privacy-conscious approach and minimizes collection of user data wherever possible.

AI Prep is intended for educational and self-learning purposes related to artificial intelligence, machine learning, programming, and related technical subjects.

1. Information Handling Overview

1.1 Personal Data

We do not require user accounts and do not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Quiz answers or written responses

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Most educational progress and activity data is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Test history
  • Scores
  • XP and levels
  • Bookmarks
  • Learning progress
  • Daily streaks
  • Current learning focus

This locally stored data remains on your device unless you:

  • Delete the app
  • Clear app storage/data
  • Reset your device

1.3 Anonymous Analytics

With your permission, AI Prep may collect anonymous usage analytics to help us understand how users interact with the app and improve the learning experience.

These analytics may include:

  • Quiz and test completion activity
  • Difficulty and topic engagement
  • Feature usage
  • Learning concept reading activity
  • Streak and progression milestones
  • Paywall and purchase events
  • App sharing actions

Analytics data is behavioral and aggregated in nature and is not intended to personally identify users.

We do not use analytics to:

  • Track typed answers or free-form input
  • Record question-by-question behavior
  • Build advertising profiles
  • Sell personal data

You may disable analytics collection at any time within the app settings.

1.4 Firebase Analytics

AI Prep uses Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, to collect anonymous app usage and technical diagnostics information.

Firebase may automatically collect certain technical information including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Approximate country or region
  • Anonymous app instance identifiers
  • Crash and performance diagnostics

We do not use Firebase Analytics for personalized advertising, remarketing, or advertising audiences.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security

1.5 Anonymous Reports

If you choose to report a question or educational issue, the app may send a report containing:

  • Question ID
  • Concept identifier
  • Optional feedback text

Reports are not linked to your identity and are used solely to improve educational quality and correctness.

1.6 AI-Assisted Educational Content

Certain educational explanations, summaries, examples, code snippets, and learning materials within the app may be generated or assisted using artificial intelligence (AI) systems.

While we strive to review and improve content quality, we do not guarantee that all educational content is fully accurate, complete, current, or error-free.

The app is intended for learning and educational support purposes only and should not be relied upon as professional, legal, financial, engineering, or academic advice.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Where applicable under GDPR or similar privacy regulations, anonymous analytics and technical diagnostics are processed:

  • Based on your consent where required
  • Or under our legitimate interest in improving app quality, reliability, and educational effectiveness

6. User Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights relating to:

  • Access to data
  • Deletion of data
  • Withdrawal of analytics consent
  • Restriction of processing

Since AI Prep does not maintain user accounts or directly identifiable profiles, many requests may already be satisfied by disabling analytics, clearing app storage, or uninstalling the app.

7. Data Security

  • HTTPS encryption is used where applicable.
  • No sensitive payment information is stored by us.

  • Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data.
