Privacy Policy — AI Prep

Last updated: May 17, 2026

AI Prep ("the app") is provided by MortalApps ("we", "us", "our"). The app is designed with a privacy-first approach and minimizes collection of user data. AI Prep is primarily intended for educational and self-learning purposes.

1. Information Handling Overview

1.1 Personal Data

We do not collect, store, or process personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The app does not require user accounts.

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Your activity within the app is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. This may include:

  • Test history
  • Scores
  • XP and levels
  • Learning progress
  • Bookmarks
  • Daily streaks
  • Current learning focus

1.3 Anonymous Reports

If you choose to report a question or educational issue, the app may send a report containing the question ID, concept identifier, and optional feedback text. Reports are not linked to any personal identifier and are used solely to improve content quality and accuracy.

1.4 AI-Assisted Educational Content

Certain educational explanations, summaries, examples, code snippets, and learning materials within the app may be generated or assisted using artificial intelligence (AI) systems.

While we strive to review and improve content quality, we do not guarantee that all educational content is fully accurate, complete, current, or error-free.

The app is intended for learning and educational support purposes only and should not be relied upon as professional, academic, legal, technical, financial, or engineering advice.

2. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

Any minimal processing of anonymous technical data (such as optional reports) is carried out under our legitimate interest in improving the quality, accuracy, and reliability of the app.

Most user data is stored locally on your device and remains until you:

We do not maintain personal user profiles or cloud-based personal learning records.

As we do not collect personal data, most rights under the GDPR (such as access, correction, portability, or deletion of personal data) do not apply.

Users may still contact us for any privacy-related concerns or questions.
