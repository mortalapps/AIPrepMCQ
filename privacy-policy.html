Privacy Policy — AI Prep

Last updated: May 27, 2026

AI Prep ("the app") is provided by MortalApps ("we", "us", "our"). The app is designed with a privacy-conscious approach and aims to minimize unnecessary collection of user data wherever possible.

AI Prep is intended for educational and self-learning purposes related to artificial intelligence, machine learning, programming, data science, and related technical subjects.

1. Information Handling Overview

1.1 Personal Data

We do not require user accounts and do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Precise location
  • Typed quiz answers or free-form educational responses

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Most educational progress and activity data is stored locally on your device. This may include:

  • Test history
  • Scores
  • XP and levels
  • Bookmarks
  • Learning progress
  • Daily streaks
  • Current learning focus
  • Premium entitlement status

This locally stored data generally remains on your device unless you:

  • Delete the app
  • Clear app storage/data
  • Reset your device

1.3 Usage Analytics

With your permission where required by applicable law, AI Prep may collect analytics and technical diagnostics information to understand app usage, improve educational features, measure engagement, support premium functionality, optimize monetization, and help sustain the app experience.

Analytics events may include:

  • Quiz and test completion activity
  • Difficulty and topic engagement
  • Learning concept reading activity
  • Progression milestones and streaks
  • Premium purchase and paywall interactions
  • Feature usage patterns
  • App sharing actions
  • App stability and crash diagnostics

Analytics data is generally behavioral and technical in nature and is not intended to directly identify individual users.

We do not intentionally use analytics to:

  • Track typed answers or free-form educational input
  • Record detailed question-by-question behavioral replay
  • Collect sensitive personal information through analytics events
  • Sell personal information to third parties

Analytics collection settings may be configurable within the app, subject to technical and platform limitations.

1.4 Firebase Analytics & Google Services

AI Prep uses Firebase Analytics and related Google services to collect usage analytics, app diagnostics, engagement metrics, conversion information, and monetization insights.

Firebase and related Google services may automatically collect technical information including:

  • Device model
  • Operating system version
  • App version
  • Approximate country or region
  • Anonymous app instance identifiers
  • Crash and performance diagnostics
  • Advertising and conversion-related identifiers where supported

Analytics data may be used to understand which features, topics, engagement patterns, or user segments are associated with:

  • Educational engagement
  • Premium conversion
  • Feature effectiveness
  • Advertising and promotion performance

Some analytics information may be used in connection with Google advertising products, including audience measurement, conversion tracking, advertising optimization, remarketing, and improving the relevance or effectiveness of promotions and advertisements.

We do not intentionally use analytics data to directly identify individual users.

Learn more:

  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Google Privacy Policy

1.5 Anonymous Reports

If you choose to report a question, error, or educational issue, the app may transmit:

  • Question ID
  • Concept identifier
  • Optional feedback text

Reports are used to improve educational quality, correctness, and app reliability.

1.6 AI-Assisted Educational Content

Certain educational explanations, summaries, examples, code snippets, and learning materials within the app may be generated or assisted using artificial intelligence (AI) systems.

While we strive to review and improve content quality, educational materials may still contain inaccuracies, omissions, ambiguities, simplified explanations, or outdated information.

The app is intended for learning and educational support purposes only and should not be relied upon as professional, financial, legal, engineering, academic, or career advice.
