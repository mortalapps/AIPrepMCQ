Privacy Policy — AI Prep MCQ

Last updated: April 17, 2026

AI Prep MCQ ("the app") is designed with a privacy-first approach. The app primarily functions offline and does not require account creation.

1. Information Handling Overview

1.1 Personal Data

The app is designed such that we do not collect or store personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

1.2 Data Stored on Your Device

Your activity within the app is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to our servers. This may include:

  • Test history and scores
  • Performance tracking data
  • Progression (XP, levels, streaks)
  • App preferences and settings
  • Purchase status stored securely on-device

2. Permissions

3. Third-Party Services

3.1 Google Play Billing

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We receive a purchase token for verification. Google Privacy Policy

3.2 Google Forms

If you choose to report a question, a Google Form may open within the app. Information submitted through the form is processed by Google and may be accessible to us as the form owner for the purpose of reviewing and improving app content.

This feature is optional and only used when you choose to submit a report.

3.3 No Analytics or Advertising SDKs

The app does not include analytics or advertising SDKs and does not use internet access for tracking or behavioral profiling.

4. Data Security

  • Data is stored locally on your device
  • Secure storage mechanisms are used where appropriate
  • Uninstalling the app removes all local data

5. Children’s Privacy

The app is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated. Continued use indicates acceptance of changes.
